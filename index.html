About YEN SPECTRA 2026

YEN SPECTRA 2026 is the annual cultural fest organized by the Department of Computer Science, Yenepoya Institute of Arts, Science, Commerce and Management. The fest provides a vibrant platform for students to showcase their creativity, artistic talents, and team spirit through a variety of exciting cultural competitions.

YEN SPECTRA celebrates creativity, talent, and healthy competition while bringing together students from different departments to showcase their abilities, foster teamwork, and create unforgettable memories.

Our Events

Discover exciting competitions that celebrate creativity, talent, and passion. Click on an event to know more.

Registration Guidelines

  • Click View Details for your preferred event and then click Register Now to complete your registration.
  • Students can participate in a maximum of three events.
  • Registration once submitted cannot be modified or cancelled.
  • For team events, only one team member should register on behalf of the entire team.
  • Ensure all participant details are entered correctly before submitting the registration.
  • Participants must carry their College ID Card during the event.
  • Participants should report to the venue 15 minutes before the scheduled event.
  • The decision of the judges and the organizing committee shall be final.
